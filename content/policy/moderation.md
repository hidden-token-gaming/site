# Moderation and appeals

How HTG staff respond when the [rules](rules.md) are broken, and how to appeal.

## The ladder

Most rule breaks move one step up the ladder each time:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Warning** — a staff member tells you what rule you broke |
| 2 | **1-hour timeout** |
| 3 | **24-hour timeout** |
| 4 | **Kick** — removed from the server; you can rejoin |
| 5 | **Ban** — permanent removal |

Staff may start further up the ladder for serious behaviour, and every action is recorded privately
in the moderation log.

## Immediate bans

These skip the ladder and go straight to a ban:

- cheating, exploits or XP farming (rule 5);
- hate speech, threats of violence, or targeted harassment;
- doxxing or sharing someone's private information or recordings (rule 7);
- any sexual content involving minors, which is also reported to Discord;
- raiding, mass spam, or scams.

## Under 18

Members found to be under 18 are removed (rule 1). This isn't a punishment and isn't recorded as a
ban; you're welcome back once you're 18.

## Appeals

- **Warnings and timeouts:** open a ticket in `#support` explaining why the decision was wrong. A
  staff member who didn't take the action reviews it.
- **Kicks and bans:** you can appeal **30 days** after the action. Because a banned account can't
  reach `#support`, ban appeals go to **[appeals@hiddentoken.com](mailto:appeals@hiddentoken.com)**. Include your Discord username,
  roughly when it happened, and why the decision should change.
- **One appeal per action.** Cheating bans (rule 5) are only reversed if the ban was a mistake,
  for example someone else's account or a misread report.
- Staff aim to answer an appeal within 14 days. The decision on an appeal is final.
