# Hidden Token Gaming

A variety gaming community for adults who like to play together. We crew up in **Sea of Thieves**,
fly ops in **Star Citizen**, seed our **War Dogs** server, run **Counter-Strike 2** PUGs and squad
up in **PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS**, with more games to come.

[Join us on Discord](https://discord.gg/6AKnEQV)

## How it works

- **Discord is home.** Pick your games, platform and region when you join, and only see the
  channels you want.
- **Get in the same game.** Each game has a [playbook](playbooks/_index.md) for ending up in the
  same crew, party, server or squad, and a finder channel to find people.
- **Play fair.** HTG is 18+ only. Cheating, exploits and XP farming are a permanent ban, everywhere.
  Read the [rules](policy/rules.md) before you join.
