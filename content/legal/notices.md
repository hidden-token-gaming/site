# Required notices

The notices each publisher requires, or that we carry by choice, copied from their source on
2026-10-02. On hiddentoken.com, the [site footer](#site-footer) carries the Star Citizen notice on
every page and links here for the rest. Each game's section says where else its notice goes. The reasons are in [publisher rules](publisher-rules.md).

Copy the notice text exactly as shown here. Don't fix the publishers' grammar, and don't shorten any
notice. **Pending legal review.**

## Star Citizen

**Required** by CIG on every fan site, org domain, blog or social page. The notice must be "open,
obvious, and can be readily seen by any visitor". It must never be smaller than other notices or
tucked away. The page must also link to the official site.

```text
This is an unofficial Star Citizen fan site, not affiliated with the Cloud Imperium group of companies. All content on this site not authored by its host or users are property of their respective owners.
```

Official site link: <https://robertsspaceindustries.com>

When showing RSI content or marks, also include the trademark notice from the Fan Kit. The line
below is read from the example image in CIG's FAQ. The Fan Kit's own text sits behind an RSI login
and hasn't been compared (*unverified*):

```text
Star Citizen®, Roberts Space Industries® and Cloud Imperium® are registered trademarks of Cloud Imperium Rights LLC
```

Where it goes:

- the site footer
- the Star Citizen playbook, pinned in `#sc-playbook` on Discord (members-only)
- pinned in the Discord Star Citizen channels

Source: [Fankit and Fandom FAQ](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006895793), "Fan Sites" and "Attribution and Credit".

## Sea of Thieves

**Required** by Microsoft wherever Sea of Thieves content (images, video, music, art) is shared. It
goes "anywhere else that makes sense so long as anyone who sees your Item will easily find this
notice". It must sit next to a link to the Game Content Usage Rules. The template, verbatim:

```text
[Name of the Microsoft Game] © Microsoft Corporation. [The title of your Item] was created under Microsoft's "Game Content Usage Rules" using assets from [Name of the Microsoft Game], and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with Microsoft.
```

Filled in for HTG:

```text
Sea of Thieves © Microsoft Corporation. The Hidden Token Gaming website was created under Microsoft's "Game Content Usage Rules" using assets from Sea of Thieves, and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with Microsoft.
```

Link: <https://www.xbox.com/en-US/developers/rules>

Microsoft's trademark guidelines also recommend a trademark footnote:

```text
Microsoft, Sea of Thieves and Xbox are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies.
```

Where it goes:

- this page, which every page of the site links to from its footer
- the Sea of Thieves playbook in `#sot-playbook` on Discord (members-only), with "This guide" as the title
- anything else that uses Sea of Thieves art, such as event graphics

Sources: [Game Content Usage Rules](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/developers/rules); [Microsoft Trademark and Brand Guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks).

## PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS

**Required** "in any materials that display the Trademark", unless PUBG says otherwise in
writing. Any other PUBG trademarks used must be added to the notice.

```text
PUBG, PLAYERUNKNOWN’S BATTLEGROUNDS and all related logos are trademarks of PUBG Corporation or its affiliates.
```

The required text still names PUBG Corporation, although the game is now published by KRAFTON,
Inc. We copy the text as written and don't update it ourselves.

Where it goes:

- this page, which every page of the site links to from its footer
- the PUBG playbook in `#pubg-playbook` on Discord (members-only)
- any page that shows PUBG API data

Source: [PUBG Developer API Terms](https://developer.pubg.com/tos), "PUBG Community Qualification and Trademark Guidelines", Content and Use Guidelines item 8.

## Counter-Strike 2 and Steam

**Not required.** Valve publishes no notice rule for fan sites or community servers. We attribute
Valve's marks anyway, using the wording from Valve's own counter-strike.net footer, without its
copyright year:

```text
Counter-Strike, Counter-Strike 2, CS:GO, and their respective logos are trademarks and/or registered trademarks of Valve Corporation.
```

If the site uses Steam sign-in or Steam data, it adds the following line, from Valve's Steam Web
API terms:

```text
Valve, Steam, the Valve logo and the Steam logo are registered trademarks of Valve Corporation.
```

That use also carries two duties from the same terms. The site must never imply a relationship with
Valve beyond "has implemented the Steam Web API". It must also have a privacy policy covering Steam
data (handbook#5).

Where it goes:

- this page, which every page of the site links to from its footer
- the Counter-Strike 2 playbook in `#cs-playbook` on Discord (members-only)

Sources: [counter-strike.net](https://www.counter-strike.net) footer; [Steam Web API Terms of Use](https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apiterms) §3, §5, §9.

## WARDOGS

**Not required.** Bulkhead and Team17 publish no fan-content policy or notice. The EULA forbids
suggesting any affiliation or endorsement, which the general line in the site footer covers.

Source: [WARDOGS EULA](https://www.wardogs.com/eula), §4.4(b).

## Site footer

The footer on every page of hiddentoken.com, ready to use. It carries only the Star Citizen notice,
which CIG requires to be readily seen by any visitor and never tucked away, and the general line,
which covers the publishers that ask for no notice. The footer also links to this page for every
other notice. Whether a linked page is enough for Microsoft's and PUBG's notices is a question
for counsel. Both lines use the same type size. Keep the link.

```markdown
This is an unofficial Star Citizen fan site, not affiliated with the Cloud Imperium group of companies. All content on this site not authored by its host or users are property of their respective owners. Official site: [robertsspaceindustries.com](https://robertsspaceindustries.com).

Hidden Token Gaming is an independent fan community. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve, Bulkhead, Team17, Microsoft, Rare, Cloud Imperium Games or KRAFTON.
```
