# About

## Where the name comes from

In the 1990s, John worked at a video arcade. When his friends came in, they said it was like he had
a "hidden token": he could play any machine he liked. That's the idea behind HTG, a place where
you always have a game to play.

The mark is two arcade tokens, with the second one hidden behind the first. There's always another
game.

## What HTG is for

Hidden Token Gaming is a variety gaming community for adults who play fair. Pick the games you
play when you join and you'll see their channels, a playbook for ending up in the same crew, party,
server or squad, and the people who play them. The line-up is on the [games page](games.md), and
the weekly sessions are on [events](events.md).

- **18+ only.** HTG is a community for adults, and we check at the door.
- **No paid perks.** Nobody can buy an advantage here, in any game.
- **Fair play.** Cheating, exploits and XP farming are a permanent ban, everywhere
  ([rules](policy/rules.md)).

## Who runs it

John founded HTG and runs it. Moderators and a lead for each game join as the community grows.
Questions go in a `#support` ticket on Discord.

## What we're building

HTG's tools are built on [gravel](https://github.com/gravel-project/gravel), an open-source
framework we're building for running game servers across many games, with linked identities and
cross-game stats. It's early: the design is done and the code is starting. HTG is where it gets
tested on a real community before it's released for others to use.

What's public today: this website ([hidden-token-gaming/site](https://github.com/hidden-token-gaming/site))
and the organisation's profile and contribution guides
([hidden-token-gaming/.github](https://github.com/hidden-token-gaming/.github)).
