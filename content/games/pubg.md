PUBG has no community servers, so HTG is where you find a duo or a squad, and where custom-match
events get organised.

## How we play

- **Find a squad** on Discord: say duo or squad, mode, region and platform. Add each other as
  friends by in-game name, and the squad leader invites from the lobby.
- **Custom matches for events.** Creating one needs BATTLEGROUNDS PLUS on the host's account;
  joining one doesn't. Signed-up members get the lobby name and password.

The step-by-step playbook is pinned in the game's Discord channel once you've joined.

## Platforms

PC players (Steam and Kakao) play together, and PlayStation 5 and Xbox players play together, but
PC and console can't squad up with each other. Say which you're on.

## Fair play

No cheats, macros, or tools that read game memory or change the UI. KRAFTON bans for them, and so
do we. More in [publisher rules](../legal/publisher-rules.md#krafton-pubg-battlegrounds).
