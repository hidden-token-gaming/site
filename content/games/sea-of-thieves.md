Sea of Thieves has no community servers, so HTG is where you find a crew. Post what ship you want
to sail and what you want to do, swap gamertags, and set sail together.

## How we crew up

- **Xbox friends, on every platform.** Everyone signs in with a Microsoft account, PC and
  PlayStation included, and invites run through Xbox friends.
- **Closed Crew.** The captain sets sail with a Closed Crew and invites the others, so a stranger
  can't take the empty slot.
- **Custom Seas for events.** One server for a whole HTG event: up to 24 players in 6 crews.
  Events are on the [events page](../events.md) and in Discord.
- **Guilds later.** HTG founds its own guild once there are members to fill it.

The step-by-step playbook is pinned in the game's Discord channel once you've joined.

## Platforms

PC (Steam and Xbox app), Xbox and PlayStation 5. Crossplay has to be on for PC and console players
to crew together.

## Fair play

Rare has zero tolerance for cheats and third-party tools, and its code of conduct forbids teaming in
competitive modes like Hourglass. We don't organise it. HTG never puts Sea of Thieves content behind
a payment. More in [publisher rules](../legal/publisher-rules.md#microsoft-and-rare-sea-of-thieves).
