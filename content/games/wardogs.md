HTG runs a War Dogs community server: **HTG WARDOGS | NA WEST | #1**, playing King of the Hill
through all three maps.

## How we play

- **Find the server** in War Dogs under **Deploy → Community**. The browser shows who's on.
- **Seed it to 20.** A match on the HTG server starts at 20 players; below that, everyone waits in
  the lobby. Members who take the seeder ping get called when the server needs players, so the
  first 20 arrive together.
- **Wardogs Wednesdays.** Every Wednesday evening (US Central) we seed the server and play. Times
  are on the [events page](../events.md).
- **Squad up in voice** on Discord once you're in.

The step-by-step playbook is pinned in the game's Discord channel once you've joined.

## Platforms and region

PC (Steam). The server is in NA West; players from anywhere are welcome.

## Fair play

**XP and cash farming is a permanent ban,** here and from Bulkhead, which tracks who joins which
servers. The HTG server's settings stay inside what Bulkhead allows, and nothing on it is sold. More
in [publisher rules](../legal/publisher-rules.md#bulkhead-and-team17-wardogs).
