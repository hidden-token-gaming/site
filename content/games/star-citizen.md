Star Citizen has no private servers and you can't pick your shard, so flying together takes a
little planning. HTG runs organised ops: sign up for a seat on a ship, party up, launch together.

## How we fly together

- **Sign up for an op.** Each op lists its ships and seats (pilot, gunners, engineer, medic).
  Take a seat, and you'll hear about the next one.
- **Party at the main menu.** Everyone waits at the main menu, the op lead invites, and the party
  leader launches first so the whole party lands on the same shard.
- **Big ops fly one party per ship,** so a split across shards never separates a crew from its
  ship.

The step-by-step playbook is pinned in the game's Discord channel once you've joined.

## Platforms

PC only. Add your crewmates as contacts by their RSI handle.

## Fair play

Nothing that injects into or modifies the game client: CIG bans for it, and so do we. Nothing paid
on HTG touches Star Citizen, as CIG's fan policy requires. More in
