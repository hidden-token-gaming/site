HTG runs Counter-Strike 2 PUGs: ten players, teams and the map veto sorted on Discord, then
everyone connects to the same server.

## How we play

- **Join a PUG** on Discord and take the PUG ping to hear when one is forming.
- **When ten are in,** teams and the map veto are sorted in Discord and every player gets the
  connect details.
- **Connect directly.** Premier and matchmaking lobbies can't join a community server, so you use
  the connect link or the console.
- **Servers:** for now a PUG plays on a server a member provides. HTG's own servers come later.

The step-by-step playbook is pinned in the game's Discord channel once you've joined.

## Platforms

PC (Steam).

## Fair play

No skin changers or inventory plugins (`!ws`, knives, gloves) on any server HTG runs or uses: Valve
permanently disables the server tokens of servers that offer them. Cheating anywhere is a
permanent ban here. More in
[publisher rules](../legal/publisher-rules.md#valve-counter-strike-2-and-steam).
