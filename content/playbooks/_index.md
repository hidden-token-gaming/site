# Playbooks

Step-by-step guides for joining other HTG members in game.

1. **Pick your games in Discord.** Each game's channels appear once you pick it during onboarding.
2. **Find people** in the game's finder channel, or ping its opt-in role.
3. **Jump in voice,** then follow the game's playbook to end up in the same crew, party, server or
   squad.
