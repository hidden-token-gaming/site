# Games

## Sea of Thieves

Crews of friends via Xbox friends and Closed Crew invites, HTG guilds, and Custom Seas for big
events. [Sea of Thieves playbook](playbooks/sea-of-thieves.md)

## Star Citizen

Organised ops: sign up for a seat, party at the main menu, launch together.
[Star Citizen playbook](playbooks/star-citizen.md)

## War Dogs

The HTG community server, **HTG WARDOGS | NA WEST | #1**. Matches start at 20 players, so seeding
matters. [War Dogs playbook](playbooks/war-dogs.md)

## Counter-Strike 2

Ten-player PUGs organised in Discord. HTG's own community servers are on the way.
[Counter-Strike 2 playbook](playbooks/counter-strike-2.md)

## PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS

Duo and squad play, and custom matches for events. [PUBG playbook](playbooks/pubg.md)
