# Games

## Sea of Thieves

Crews of friends via Xbox friends and Closed Crew invites, HTG guilds, and Custom Seas for big
events. Members: the playbook is in `#sot-playbook`.

## Star Citizen

Organised ops: sign up for a seat, party at the main menu, launch together. Members: the playbook is in `#sc-playbook`.

## War Dogs

The HTG community server, **HTG WARDOGS | NA WEST | #1**. Matches start at 20 players, so seeding
matters. Members: the playbook is in `#wd-playbook`.

## Counter-Strike 2

Ten-player PUGs organised in Discord. HTG's own community servers are on the way. Members: the playbook is in `#cs-playbook`.

## PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS

Duo and squad play, and custom matches for events. Members: the playbook is in `#pubg-playbook`.
